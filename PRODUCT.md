# RabittoPetStore — Admin Frontend

## Product Purpose

Internal admin dashboard for managing a pet store operation. Staff-facing tool for scheduling, employee management, appointment tracking, and service configuration. Not a customer-facing app.

## Users

**Primary**: Pet store managers and admins. Experienced with the store's workflow, check this system daily or multiple times per day. Access from a workstation or office laptop. Task-oriented, efficiency-focused. Not casual users.

## Register

product

## Brand

**Black & Gold Elegance**

Palette: Black (#000000), Prussian Blue (#14213d), Gold (#fca311), Alabaster Grey (#e5e5e5), White (#ffffff).

Tone: Authoritative, clean, professional. The gold accent signals action and priority. Deep navy and black convey gravity and trust. Not playful, not consumer-app cheerful.

## Anti-References

- Pastel / friendly consumer apps (Shopify, Squarespace)
- Neon dark-mode developer tools
- Overcrowded data dashboards (Grafana-style overload)
- Generic SaaS blue-and-white templates

## Strategic Principles

- Clarity over cleverness. Staff need to get in and get to work.
- Consistency is an affordance. Same patterns, same vocabulary, screen to screen.
- Gold accent reserved for primary actions only. Not decoration.
- Dark theme. The palette earns it; the context (focused, purposeful work environment) confirms it.
